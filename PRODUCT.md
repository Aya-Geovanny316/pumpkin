# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS/JS, preserved because the repository is a single-page static site

## Users

The recipient is the author's romantic partner, reading the page on a phone or desktop.

## Product Purpose

A personal romantic letter presented as a memorable digital keepsake. Success means the recipient can open the letter, read the full message, revisit the photos, and reach the playlist without friction.

## Positioning

The page is a private, authored love letter rather than a generic public landing page; its value comes from the supplied words, memories, images, video, and playlist.

## Operating Context

The experience is read and explored in one sitting, beginning with a reveal interaction and continuing through the message, memory images, and music link.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Keep the existing Spanish texts, local images, local video, and Spotify playlist.
- Keep the page static and deployable without a build step.
- Improve the visual system, hierarchy, responsive behavior, accessibility, and interaction quality.

## Brand Commitments

Warm, intimate, sincere, and personal. Existing content and media are binding assets.

## Evidence on Hand

`index.html`, `calabaza.png`, `OIP.png`, `pumpkin.jpeg`, `smile.jpeg`, `us.jpeg`, and `YourName.mp4` are supplied repository assets. The page also contains the supplied Spotify playlist embed/link. No testimonials, business claims, or external brand system should be invented.

## Product Principles

- Let the personal letter be the visual centerpiece.
- Make the reveal feel intentional and calm.
- Keep the full message easy to read on a phone.
- Use the supplied media as evidence of the relationship, not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, and non-blocking media behavior are required.
